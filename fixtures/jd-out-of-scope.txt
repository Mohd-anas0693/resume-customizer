Staff Nurse — Intensive Care Unit
Grace Memorial Hospital — Mumbai, India (rotating shifts)

Position summary
Grace Memorial Hospital is recruiting Staff Nurses for our 24-bed Intensive Care
Unit. You will deliver direct nursing care to critically ill adult patients,
working as part of a multidisciplinary team alongside intensivists, respiratory
therapists and physiotherapists.

Key responsibilities
- Provide total patient care for critically ill patients at a 1:1 or 1:2 ratio.
- Monitor and record vital signs, and escalate deterioration promptly.
- Manage patients on mechanical ventilation, including ventilator checks,
  suctioning, and weaning protocols under medical direction.
- Administer medication, including titrated vasoactive infusions, and maintain
  accurate medication administration records.
- Perform triage and assist with admissions from the emergency department.
- Maintain complete and accurate charting in the patient record.
- Support families, and participate in end-of-life care discussions.

Essential requirements
- Registered Nurse licence with a valid registration from the Indian Nursing
  Council or a State Nursing Council. This is a statutory requirement and
  cannot be waived.
- B.Sc. Nursing or GNM qualification from a recognised institution.
- Minimum 3 years of post-registration clinical nursing experience, of which at
  least 2 years must be in an ICU or high-dependency unit.
- Current BLS certification (basic life support).
- Ability to work rotating shifts, including nights and weekends.

Desirable
- ACLS certification.
- Experience with CRRT (continuous renal replacement therapy).
