Backend Engineer
Northwind Systems — Pune, India (hybrid)

About the role
We are hiring a Backend Engineer to work on the services behind our billing and
reporting products. You will own API endpoints end to end, from the database
schema through to the HTTP contract, and work closely with two front-end
engineers who consume what you build.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain REST APIs in Node.js and TypeScript.
- Design and evolve PostgreSQL schemas, including writing and reviewing migrations.
- Write unit and integration tests; we expect new code to arrive with tests.
- Review pull requests from other engineers and keep the codebase tidy.
- Help run the services you build, with support from our platform team.

Requirements
- Minimum 2 years of professional experience building backend web services.
- Strong JavaScript and TypeScript. We write all new services in TypeScript.
- Hands-on experience with Node.js, ideally with Express or a similar framework.
- Working knowledge of SQL and relational schema design; PostgreSQL preferred.
- Experience writing automated tests (we use Jest).
- Comfortable with Git-based workflows and code review.

Nice to have
- Docker, and some familiarity with container-based deployment.
- Exposure to React, so you can debug across the stack.
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines.

What we offer
Hybrid working from our Pune office, a learning budget, and health cover for you
and your dependants.
