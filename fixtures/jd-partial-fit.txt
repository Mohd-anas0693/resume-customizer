Senior Platform Engineer
Harbor Cloud — Bengaluru, India (on-site 3 days/week)

The team
Harbor Cloud runs the internal developer platform used by every product team in
the company. We own the Kubernetes clusters, the deployment tooling, and the
observability stack that around 180 engineers depend on daily.

What you'll do
- Operate and extend our multi-tenant Kubernetes platform.
- Write platform tooling and controllers in Go.
- Manage all infrastructure as code with Terraform.
- Own the CI/CD pipelines that ship roughly 300 deploys a week.
- Define SLOs and build dashboards and alerts in Prometheus and Grafana.
- Act as a technical lead: mentor two or three engineers and set direction for
  the platform roadmap.

Requirements
- 6+ years of software or infrastructure engineering experience, including at
  least 3 years running Kubernetes in production.
- Strong Go. Most of our tooling is written in Go and you will be reviewing it.
- Deep Terraform experience; you should be comfortable owning module design.
- Solid understanding of Linux networking and container runtimes.
- Experience as a technical lead or senior engineer on a platform team.
- PostgreSQL and Node.js familiarity is useful, since some legacy services are
  Node-based and we still operate their databases.

Nice to have
- Prometheus, Grafana, or another observability stack at scale.
- Experience with service meshes.
